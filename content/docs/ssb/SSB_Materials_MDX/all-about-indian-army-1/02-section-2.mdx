Variants held with Indian Armed forces initially Searcher Mark-I,II. Nishant and Rustom (Indigenously developed) 

DRONE : Armed with lethal weapons it is pilotless airplane used in target practice. Depends upon pre-programmed f light plan that actually controls it, hence Drone can also be called as UAV. # Future developments 

INTEGRATED BATTLE GROUPS: IBGs are brigade sized, agile, self-suf ficient combat formation, which can swiftly launch strikes against an adversary in case of hostilities. Each IBG troop strength of around 5000, bigger than a brigade(3000-3500) but smaller than a Division (10000-12000). These will have a mix of every arm and service like infantry, Artillery, Engineers, Signals, air Defence and others. Army has decided to reconstitute its standing forces into 12 modernize IBG’s.
