&lt;mark&gt;C. Naag&lt;/mark&gt; 

&lt;mark&gt;D. Akash&lt;/mark&gt; 

**&lt;mark&gt;Answer: B. Pinaka&lt;/mark&gt;** &lt;mark&gt;- An upgraded version of India's indigenously developed Pinaka guided rocket system was successfully test-fired from Odisha coast on 19 December 2019. The rocket aims to boost the Army's artillery power. It was fired from the Proof and Experimental Establishment firing test range at Chandipur.
