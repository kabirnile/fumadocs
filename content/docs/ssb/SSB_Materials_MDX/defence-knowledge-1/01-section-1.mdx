#### **INDIAN DEFENCE SYSTEM** 

&lt;mark&gt;The president of India is the supreme commander of the Indian Defence System. The whole administrative control of the Armed forces lies in the Ministry of Defence. Indian Defence System has been divided into three services –&lt;/mark&gt; **&lt;mark&gt;Army, Navy and Air force.&lt;/mark&gt;**
