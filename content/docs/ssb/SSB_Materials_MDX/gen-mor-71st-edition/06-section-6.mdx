###### **Practical Use in Classrooms** 

Techniques like flipped classrooms, simulations, debates, and project work help students actively learn and apply concepts in engaging ways. 

###### **Challenges to Implementation** 

Experiential learning may not align well with exam patterns. Factors like language, resources, or student readiness can also affect success. 

###### **Conclusion** 

Despite challenges, experiential learning fosters life skills and deeper understanding. It can transform education when used thoughtfully. 

To improve education, we must shift from rote learning to experiential methods that promote critical thinking and problem-solving. Developing essential life skills like communication, emotional intelligence, and digital literacy is key to preparing students for the real world. Teachers should receive continuous training in modern teaching methods and the effective use of technology. Exam pressure must be reduced through concept-based and continuous assessment. Education should be made more equitable by ensuring quality resources and access for all, especially in rural and underprivileged areas. 

Technology should be used to enhance, not replace, classroom learning, with a focus on inclusivity and access. Teaching in mother tongues and addressing the needs of diverse learners can make education more inclusive. Curricula must also align with employability by including vocational training and real-world applications. Encouraging research, innovation, and creativity at all levels will help build a knowledge-based society. Finally, effective implementation of policies like NEP 2020, along with transparent and accountable governance, is essential for meaningful education reform.
